The problem

You bought AI. You didn't change how you work.

Most creative organizations run a production model designed before the internet, with AI bolted on the side. The subscriptions are paid. The pilots are done. The work still moves the way it did ten years ago: briefs buried in inboxes, reviews trapped in meetings, a week lost between every draft.

The tools were never the bottleneck.

The workflow is.

Method

Four moves. In this order.

  1. 01 / Map

    Trace the real work

    I sit inside the process and follow how work actually moves: who touches it, where it waits, where judgment happens, and where it's just friction.

  2. 02 / Redesign

    Redraw the line

    I rebuild the workflow so AI absorbs the friction and people keep the judgment. Thinking comes before prompting. Every time.

  3. 03 / Build

    Ship the machine

    I build the tools, agents, and templates the new workflow runs on. Systems your team logs into on Monday.

  4. 04 / Embed

    Make it stick

    Adoption is where transformations die. I train the team, design the habits, and stay until the new way is simply the way.

About

Making people believe is what I've done for three decades, across advertising, strategy, writing, and film. That's the part most AI consultants skip, and it's why most rollouts stall. I know which parts of the craft to protect and which parts were always just friction.
