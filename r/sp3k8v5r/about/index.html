About

A venture studio and multi-family office, privately owned and founder-led.

Steinberg Protocol is a multi-disciplinary venture studio and multi-family office, privately owned and founder-led.

Our work spans venture building and capital stewardship: first capital, strategic guidance and long-term partnership for entrepreneurs across mobility, hospitality, deep technology and the ecological transition. Our founders are practising operators, not portfolio managers, and whether backing a single venture or an entire ecosystem of them, they do so as long-term insiders. We stay embedded in projects from incubation through acceleration, never handing them off along the way.

Our structure is unusual for a firm our size. We are not organised around a single sector or fund mandate. Instead, we are organised around conviction, applied the same way to every venture we back. This reflects our belief that lasting impact cannot happen without risk, patience and, above all, personal commitment. It shows in a portfolio that spans oceans, skies and hospitality, principally across Europe, Southeast Asia and the Middle East.

Guillaume Catala

Founder, CEO · Paris, Jakarta

Guillaume Catala has spent the past three decades building ventures across a dozen countries — a French-Australian entrepreneur who has made a career of operating at the intersection of culture, commerce, and government.

Before founding Steinberg Protocol, Guillaume built East West Synergy, a Jakarta-based production house he launched in 2014 to bridge Indonesia's creative economy with international markets — work that included award-winning films and years spent co-initiating bilateral creative-economy programmes between Indonesia and the world beyond it. That same instinct for cross-cultural bridge-building now shapes Steinberg Protocol: he sits on the board of La French Tech Indonesia, works closely with government agencies, and contributes to G20 entrepreneurial programmes — relationships built over 25-plus years living and operating across eight countries.

He founded Steinberg Protocol as an impact venture studio and multi-family office, and leads Odyssea, the group's subsidiary dedicated to deep tech and frontier mobility. Guillaume is driven by a commitment to sustainability and social innovation — the conviction that entrepreneurship, applied with patience and personal commitment, can serve the common good.

He shares his time between Paris and Jakarta.
