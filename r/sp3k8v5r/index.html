How we decide

We invest by conviction, not category.

01

We go where others don't.

If an idea already has a crowded field of believers, we're probably not the ones building it with you.

02

We think in decades, not years.

Legacy enablers and long-term insiders, rather than quarter-to-quarter operators.

03

We measure impact alongside return.

People, planet and profit, each a condition of the other two.

04

Every venture strengthens the others.

We build ecosystems. Diversity, to us, is also resilience.
