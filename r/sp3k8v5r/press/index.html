Ouest-France · November 2022 · Aqualines

Fully electric, this future boat flies as fast as a high-speed train

Ouest-France explores Aqualines' fully electric ground-effect craft, which leaves port like a boat, then lifts onto a cushion of high-pressure air to fly 1.5 to 4 metres above the sea at the speed of a high-speed train. Designed to glide without disturbing marine life, the technology was set to scale from a two-to-four-seat prototype to a family of eight vessels serving routes now covered by ferries and small aircraft.

Les Echos · August 2022 · Aqualines

Aqualines designs the "high-speed train of the seas"

Les Echos examines the Naviplane, Aqualines' electric ground-effect vessel: two front propellers drive air beneath the hull to lift it onto a cushion above the waves, while a rear propeller provides thrust. The planned family ran from a nine-metre, two-to-four-seat prototype cruising at 120 km/h to a 300-passenger craft reaching 320 km/h, built for sea crossings of 50 to 500 km such as Tallinn–Helsinki, which would shrink from two and a half hours by ferry to under thirty minutes.
