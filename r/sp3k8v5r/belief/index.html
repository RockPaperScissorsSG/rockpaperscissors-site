Our belief

We invest where the future begins.

01

Humanity faces a polycrisis.

We are living through the fastest acceleration in human history, and its paradoxes have never been sharper.

Demographic and technological booms are reshaping civilisation while the ground shifts underneath them: biodiversity collapse, climate disruption, a series of regional conflicts, and security risks that now reach into supply chains, borders, oceans and orbit. These pressures compound. Each one raises the cost of solving the others.

A polycrisis resists incremental fixes. It calls for disruptive solutions, designed and financed and deployed at the speed the problem is moving. A desirable future and a secure one are the same project.

02

The transition is the opportunity.

The instinct in moments like this is to hold back. We'd rather build.

Most of this portfolio finds its opening in the ecological transition: a chance to progress and thrive as a society rather than a cost to absorb. The solutions exist, and they need capital, operators and time to reach the scale the problem demands.

We invest with the conviction that this future can be built. What we back today is the legacy our children will thrive on.

03

We invest by conviction, not category.

Our portfolio spans oceans and skies, cities and the frontier beyond, and the businesses that make daily life better along the way.

That wide range is the whole point. The future has never arrived cleanly in one industry at a time, and we are not willing to sit out the ones we didn't predict.

This is how we decide which ones are worth building.

01

We go where others don't.

If an idea already has a crowded field of believers, we're probably not the ones building it with you. We're drawn to founders who see a frontier nobody else has bothered to walk toward, sometimes literally, into the ocean or the cosmos.

02

We think in decades, not years.

Legacy enablers and long-term insiders, rather than quarter-to-quarter operators. An idea is only valuable once it has been transformed, and long-lasting transformation demands time, resources, energy and a tolerance for risk.

03

We measure impact alongside return.

A triple bottom line venture builder: people, planet and profit, each a condition of the other two. Returns matter, and so does what a venture leaves behind: resilience, regeneration, and the capacity to keep operating when conditions turn hostile.

04

Every venture strengthens the others.

We build ecosystems. Every venture we back should be able to lend something to the ecosystem: a technology, a network, a lesson learned the hard way. Diversity, to us, is also resilience.

We build a future pioneers imagine.

If an idea clears that bar, we don't just write a cheque. We back it with first capital, strategic guidance, and a community that already knows how to walk the same path: from incubation to acceleration, seed to legacy.
