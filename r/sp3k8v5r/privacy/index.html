Who we are

This website is run by Steinberg Protocol, Singapore Business Federation, #14-04 | 160 Robinson Road, Singapore 068914. Questions about this policy or about your personal data can be sent to upwards@steinberg.global.

What we collect

This site does not use advertising, tracking or analytics cookies, and it does not ask you to create an account.

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you give it, and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply and to follow up on the conversation you started.

Like every website, our hosting provider records basic technical information when a page is requested, such as your IP address, browser type and the page visited. These logs are kept for security and to keep the site running, and are deleted after a short period.

Newsletter

If you subscribe to our newsletter, you sign up on Substack, which handles your email address under its own privacy policy. You can unsubscribe at any time from the link in every email.

Embedded content and links

Some pages show videos hosted by Vimeo or YouTube, or link to press articles and the websites of our portfolio companies. When you play a video or follow a link, that service may collect data under its own policy.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run this site and our correspondence, or where the law requires it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act and the EU General Data Protection Regulation, you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Write to upwards@steinberg.global and we will respond within 30 days. In the EU you may also complain to your local data protection authority, in France the CNIL.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date above.
