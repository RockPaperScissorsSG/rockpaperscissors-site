IBP Bio — The 100% Organic B2B platform

IBP Bio is a B2B platform for organic and natural products. Suppliers list their brands and catalogues; buyers post what they are looking for; a matching engine cross-references the two and surfaces the pairings worth a conversation.

Eight product families run through it, from food and ingredients to cosmetics, agricultural inputs and equipment. Producers, processors, laboratories and farmers on one side; distributors, supermarkets, specialist retailers, pharmacies and hospitality groups on the other.

Human qualification sits at the centre. Profiles are vetted before they circulate, sourcing requirements are qualified by the team before they reach a supplier, and the relationship is followed after the introduction. A directory finds names. This finds counterparties.

Regional economic development agencies have partnered with the platform to open export markets for their members, among them Clusterbio Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, Bretagne Commerce International and AD'OCC.

Europe buys organic. The producers are dispersed. IBP Bio is the route between them.
