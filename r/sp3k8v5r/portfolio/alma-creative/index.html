A global entertainment powerhouse

Alma Creative designs and produces large-scale live shows: permanent destinations, resident productions, and formats built to travel.

It brings together two houses with thirty years each behind them. ECA2 handles production — special effects, scenography, water and light engineering. Its work includes the Burj Khalifa New Year's Eve show for four consecutive years, Kiss of the Sea in Phú Quốc, the world's largest permanent water projection theatre, and the aquatic spectacular at Expo 2025 Osaka. Wings of Time has run nightly in Sentosa since 2014.

Peparini Studios handles artistic direction, under Giuliano Peparini: opera, ballet, seven seasons of Italian prime-time television, and the 2025 reimagining of House of Dancing Water in Macau.

Their clients have included Olympic organising committees, World Expos, and the operators behind the world's major entertainment destinations. Between them: 150 shows, 420 million spectators, 20 countries.

Alma Creative turns cultural identity into shows the rest of the world will pay to see.
