Ama-Lurra Resort, Gili Air

On the sunset side of a car-free island, Ama-Lurra runs on nothing but its own sun. Opened in 2023, it was one of the first resorts in Indonesia built to run fully on solar power, with close to zero carbon emissions. It's built from local timber and local craft: an architecture that doesn't try to improve on the island, but only tries not to get in its way.

This is the kind of project we build toward: a real risk, taken early, in a market that hadn't yet asked for it. Three years in, Ama-Lurra's twelve villas hold a resilient 76–83% occupancy, in a region where tourism is growing more than 30% a year and Lombok's airport keeps expanding to meet it. In 2026 it relaunches with Iluna, a new beachfront bar and restaurant. By 2027: twenty villas, twelve suites, a spa, a yoga shala, a botanical garden: the same seed, twelve villas further along.

A small island. No cars. A team of forty who already know how to run the place. A future pioneers imagine, already taking shape, one villa at a time.
