Hunter Motorcycles - The future is Indonesian

Every Hunter motorcycle is hand-built in Bali, by Indonesians, for Indonesian roads. No import badge, no borrowed platform: mid-capacity electric and combustion bikes assembled frame by frame, fitted with the kind of components most brands save for a price point twice as high.

This is the bet we like to make: an industry Indonesia has never built for itself, taken on by people who grew up riding what the rest of the world sent them. What began in a Jimbaran workshop has outgrown it: production is moving to a full plant in Citeureup, West Java, on the edge of Greater Jakarta, with the Bali site turning into Hunter's R&D centre. Eight models on the road, electric already in the range, and a customer list that runs from weekend riders to the Indonesian police and army.

A factory, not a franchise. A brand built by the people who ride it. And a future pioneers imagine - this time, with an engine in it.
