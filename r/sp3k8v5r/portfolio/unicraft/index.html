Unicraft

Unicraft invests, then moves in. It takes a position alongside founders building impact technology, and stays close enough to shape the product from the core. Capital is the entry point; the contribution comes after.

The studio side supplies what most seed rounds leave founders to solve alone: design and growth talent, assembled on demand, working as a second founding team. Brands and digital products get built in the room, not commissioned from outside it.

The name holds the method. Unity, for the commitment of a founder rather than a shareholder. Craft, for ventures made the way a craftsman makes things — by hand, to a standard.
