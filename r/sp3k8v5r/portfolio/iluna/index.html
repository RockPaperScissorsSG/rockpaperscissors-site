Iluna - The Pause

Iluna opened in August 2026 on the quiet sunset shoreline of Gili Air, in front of Ama-Lurra eco-resort. It’s the first beach lounge on the island. It offers a place to rest: the sun, the sea, the quiet, and an afternoon to enjoy the ultimate beach experience.

The name comes from the Basque language, as does the hotel's. “Ilunabar” is the time of day that moves toward dusk, the hour when the light begins to go. It is the moment the whole place is built around: sunset.

In practice that means private daybeds set on the sand, linen cushions, natural sail canopies for shade, and the sound of the sea close by. The lounge opens at ten in the morning and runs late, walk-ins welcome, daybeds worth reserving. Nobody is moved along.

There is French discipline in this kitchen, and an island's appetite for improvisation. What arrives from the sea that morning sets the terms; the technique simply knows what to do about it. The menu changes with the catch and the season, and each plate is a quiet still life, made to be sat with. At the bar, cold pressings, fresh citrus and island spirits go into the most considered cocktail list on Gili Air, built slowly and served without hurry.

Then the light does what it came to do. The heat lets go, the sun drops toward the water, and the day exhales. Everything about the place — the orientation, the shade, the pace, the timing of the menu — is arranged around being there when it happens.

Iluna sells neither food nor drinks, but a feeling: the rare luxury of an afternoon that belongs entirely to you.
