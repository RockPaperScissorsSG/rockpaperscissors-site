Everything That Mattered Deserves To Be Told Forever

EterniTales, Transtime Publishing’s platform, is the guardian of children’s memories and dreams — a bridge between a child’s imagination and their emotional heritage. More than a personalised book, each creation turns what matters most (a precious moment, a budding passion, a dream in the making) into a story to keep for life: the ephemeral, transformed into an object of transmission.

Here the child is no longer a spectator: they become the hero and the author of their own legend. Families shape the story together — a birthday, a grandparent’s bond, a first journey, an astronaut dream — and an AI-powered personalisation tool, in service of dreams, memories and emotions, brings it to life as a beautifully illustrated book. Previewed and refined until it feels exactly right, then delivered as a fine bound edition made to be read at the edge of the bed and treasured for twenty years.

Every story is anchored in one of three pillars: founding moments, turning real events into legend; bonds that build, making visible the attachment between children, parents, grandparents and siblings; and imaginaries, nurturing the passions, aspirations and dreams that build a child’s confidence.

Book after book, each family builds its Life Library, a growing collection of its best memories and its dreams in the making. Not passive nostalgia, but anchoring: preserving what is precious to nourish the future. The photo album of a new generation, where memories and dreams become living stories.
