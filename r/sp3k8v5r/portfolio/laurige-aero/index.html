Laurige Aero · Luxury at Altitude

Laurige Aero provides helicopter and aircraft cabin refurbishment, combining technical expertise with a rare degree of flexibility and responsiveness. The house works to a single standard: what leaves the workshop must satisfy aeronautical certification and an owner who has spent a lifetime among fine things.

The craft comes from leather. Laurige has been a French leather house since 1949, and the disciplines it has learned over that span — the choice of a skin, the patience of a hand-finished seam, the matching of a colour by eye — are the same ones a cabin demands. Only the surface has changed. The work covers full reupholstery, leather restoration, bespoke finishes, panels and trim, and carpets: every surface a passenger sees and touches, at close quarters, for the length of a flight.

Speed is part of the offer. An aircraft on the ground earns nothing, so Laurige Aero operates as a mobile team, moving quickly to meet the specific needs and high expectations of each client, and minimising downtime.

That expertise has been validated on sensitive and prestigious projects, carried out for heads of state and VVIP operators. Work of this kind demands a mastery of aeronautical standards and close collaboration with leading industrial partners, integrating cutting-edge design elements and functionality into a certified airframe — and it demands discretion.
