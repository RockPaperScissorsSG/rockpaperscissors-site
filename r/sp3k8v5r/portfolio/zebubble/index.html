ZeBubble - The screen visor that speaks for your brand.

ZeBubble is a cardboard visor that sits over a laptop screen without tools or adhesive, shielding it from stray light and giving the user privacy. The engineering is origami: folded card locked onto itself, no glue and no screws, with pre-cut tabs giving a single size that fits screens from 13 to 15.6 inches. Assembled or removed in twenty seconds.

Four printable faces turn the object into an advertising surface that travels with its audience: open the laptop on a train, a café or an open space and the message opens with it. Each visor accumulates thousands of impressions over its life, wherever the computer is opened, and the QR code printed on the card lets the advertiser count who acted on it - something billboards have never managed.

Patented, designed and manufactured in France, on recycled and PEFC paper with water-based inks and varnishes.

The screen protector is the product. The media is the business.
