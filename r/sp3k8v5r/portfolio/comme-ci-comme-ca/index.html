A few words on the founder: Willy Stevens.

Forty years in leather, and the client list reads like a directory of the houses that take it most seriously: Chopard, Cartier, Morabito, Ted Lapidus, Loxwood, Guerlain. Jewellers, perfumers, couturiers — clients who notice a stitch out of line, and who come back only if it never is.

Comme-Çi Comme-Ça - The Luxury of Infinite Conscious Choices

There is a particular kind of vanity in owning a wardrobe of handbags, and a particular kind of waste. Comme-Çi Comme-Ça proposes something simpler and rather more clever: one bag, and a series of flaps that change it entirely.

The flaps are interchangeable and reversible, in a range of colours and textures, and the arithmetic works quickly in your favour. Two flaps become four looks. Add a third and the possibilities multiply again. The bag beneath stays the same — chic, structured, the sort of thing that goes anywhere — while the face it presents to the world shifts with the season, the outfit, or the hour.

One bag, one shelf, and the range of a collection. Conscious fashion is often framed as restraint. Here it arrives as invention: the same object, endlessly reconsidered, and the styling left entirely to the person carrying it.

Made in Indonesia.
