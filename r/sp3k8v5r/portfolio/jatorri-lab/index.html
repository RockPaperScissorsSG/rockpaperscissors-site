Jatorri Lab

Jatorri means origin in Basque — a name the group chose deliberately for its cultural arm: a publishing house and an artist incubator, run on the conviction that a book or a record can move further than a balance sheet.

The publishing side backs authors with a thesis. Christian Buchet's Dare the Sea argues that France could become the world's leading economic power if it dares its own maritime revolution — and it reached 1,700 political and economic decision-makers, each copy sent with a letter from the author. A book, placed on the right desks, is a policy instrument.

Beyond Java was selected for the 70th anniversary of France–Indonesia diplomatic relations. Nusa Ina foregrounds Indonesia's cultural wealth by archiving the knowledge, practices, and environmental stewardship of its indigenous communities across the archipelago. Psycolorist turns colour into a method for reading ourselves, backed by scientific research. Sovereign 3.0 dives deep into the world of distributed ledger technologies, exploring their adoption by governments and uncovering the keys behind a new way to govern.

The incubator side produces artists: Arise fusing electronic music, song, and contemporary dance; Mac Guffin working a cinematic vintage and trip-hop register with international guests; Yawdel built as an anonymous EDM persona, origin undisclosed by design. Cinematic, cross-disciplinary, built over years rather than seasons.

Every book, every record adds a line to the soft power of a country.
