Adina - Reimagining Senior Living With Five-Star Care.

Most of the care an older person needs does not require a doctor or a nurse. It requires someone to sit with, something to do, a good meal, a reason to get dressed. Adina is built on that observation, and on the sentence that follows from it: more hospitality, less hospital.

Adina is the residences brand of the group, and provides design, technical advice, marketing and operational services for premium seniors' communities across Southeast Asia, alongside Living Well Seniors Communities. Its mission is to promote the health and wellbeing of residents in communities that are vibrant and active.

The residences are lifestyle resorts delivering five-star care — architecturally designed, hotel-style, staffed by trained professionals. Concierge services and 24-hour care sit alongside chef-prepared meals, a premium lounge, pool and gymnasium, private cinema, karaoke rooms, a beautician, cafés and medical consulting suites. Family and friends can stay too, which is the point: a home surrounded by the people who make it one.

Behind the hospitality is a specific model of care. Adina's Personal Care Team is trained through the Asosiasi Seniors Living Indonesia and works under nursing supervision when an emergency calls for it. Every residence is designed to take a live-in Personal Care Attendant should one become necessary, and to adapt as a resident's needs change. Care arrives as a continuum rather than a threshold, in an environment that is warm and inviting.

Delivering that takes more than nurses and railings. Seniors living sits across design, construction, sales and rental, medical, food and beverage, regulatory affairs, furniture and fit-out, and hospitality — which is why Adina draws on property and seniors care expertise alongside advisory support from more than twenty-five years at the executive level in international hospitality.
